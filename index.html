Digital Audio Workstations

    • Ardour: ardour.org

    • Unless you compile the software yourself ever 10 min audio stops working.

    • Zrythm: zrythm.org

    • 25 track limit on free tier.

    • Next Studio: nextstudio-daw.org

    • No catch its just a D.A.W. Honestly I recomend Next Studio over the other options.
